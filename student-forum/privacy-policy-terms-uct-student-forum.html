Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the UCT Student Forum, we collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Account Information: Your UCT email address, display name, faculty, year of study, and profile information you choose to provide.
  • Content: Posts, comments, messages, study group contributions, and other content you create on the platform.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the forum, including pages viewed, time spent, and features used.
  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide, maintain, and improve the forum experience:

  • Authenticate your UCT student status
  • Display your content and activity on the forum
  • Send notifications about replies, upvotes, and announcements
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features
  • Enforce community guidelines and terms of service
  • Protect against spam, abuse, and security threats

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • With other students: Your posts, comments, and profile information (except email) are visible to verified UCT students.
  • Anonymous posting: When you post anonymously, your identity is hidden from other users but remains visible to moderators for enforcement purposes.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our community.

4. Data Retention

We retain your account information and content as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your posts and comments may remain visible but attributed to "Deleted User." You may request complete data deletion by contacting our support team.

5. Your Rights

As a UCT student, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data (available in Dashboard → Settings → Data)
  • Opt out of marketing communications

6. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL) to protect data transmission. All passwords are hashed and salted. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@uctstudentforum.com or through the forum's support system.

Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help us remember your preferences, analyze site traffic, and improve your experience.

Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the forum to function. These include session cookies (keep you logged in), CSRF tokens (security), and load balancer cookies. Cannot be disabled.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings such as theme preference (light/dark), language, and notification preferences. Expire after 1 year.
  • Analytics Cookies: Used to understand how students interact with the forum (e.g., which pages are most popular). We use privacy-focused analytics that anonymize IP addresses.
  • Functionality Cookies: Remember your viewed posts and saved drafts. These improve your browsing experience.

Third-Party Cookies

We do not use third-party advertising cookies. Our analytics are self-hosted to protect your privacy. Some embedded content (e.g., YouTube videos in posts) may set their own cookies — we recommend reviewing their policies separately.

Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View existing cookies and delete them
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies from specific sites
  • Clear all cookies when closing the browser

Please note: Disabling essential cookies will prevent you from staying logged in and may break forum functionality.

Cookie Consent

When you first visit the forum, you will see a cookie consent banner. By continuing to use the forum, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Cookie Policy as we add new features. We will notify users of significant changes via forum announcements.

For questions about cookies, contact privacy@uctstudentforum.com
