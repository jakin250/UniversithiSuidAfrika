About UNISA Student Forum

Building a stronger, more connected campus community — one conversation at a time.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower every UNISA student with a safe, inclusive, and collaborative platform to share knowledge, seek help, and build meaningful connections that enhance their academic journey and campus experience.

👁️

Our Vision

To become the central hub for student collaboration at UNISA — where every voice is heard, every question finds an answer, and every student feels supported.

✨ Our Core Values

🤝

Collaboration

We believe students achieve more when they work together. Our platform is designed to foster teamwork and peer-to-peer learning.

🛡️

Safety & Trust

All users are verified UNISA students. Our moderation team ensures a respectful, harassment-free environment for everyone.

🌍

Inclusivity

Every student, regardless of background, faculty, or year of study, is welcome and valued in our community.

💡

Innovation

We continuously improve based on student feedback, adding features like study groups, tags, and mobile accessibility.
